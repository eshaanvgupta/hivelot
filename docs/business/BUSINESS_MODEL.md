# HIVELOT Commercialization & Unit Economics
**Sustainable Web3 Commerce Infrastructure for QIE Ecosystem**

---

## 1. Revenue Streams

HIVELOT generates diversified, transparent revenue through four channels:

1. **Protocol Settlement Fees (0.75% Take Rate):**
   - Automatically deducted from winning supplier payouts upon verified delivery.
   - For a 100,000 QUSDC batch procurement, generates 750 QUSDC for `ProtocolTreasury`.
2. **Merchant Pro Subscriptions ($29/month):**
   - Advanced automated bidding APIs, historical clearing price analytics, priority listing.
3. **Enterprise Organization Workspaces ($79/month):**
   - Team procurement permissions, multi-sig department budgets, automated purchase export reporting.
4. **Custom Enterprise Integrations:**
   - Dedicated procurement contracts and private arbitrator network integrations for corporate procurement departments.

---

## 2. Initial Category Focus

To maximize near-term traction, HIVELOT prioritizes standardized, non-perishable categories with homogeneous demand specifications:
- **IT & Enterprise Hardware:** Routers, switches, docking stations, standardized server peripherals.
- **Office Equipment & Supplies:** Ergonomic chairs, bulk office paper, high-throughput thermal labels.
- **Pooled Digital Licensures:** Permitted corporate SaaS seats with group pricing concessions.
